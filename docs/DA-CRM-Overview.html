DA-CRM

Our own customer platform, built inside DA Platform, replacing HubSpot's CRM, ticketing and workflow role for the team that supports roughly 2,100 franchise dealerships.

Project overview for Allan and the COO · October 20, 2026 · Phase 1 built (branch crm-phase-1, not yet deployed)

What DA-CRM replaces, and why

Today, dealer relationships live in three places. Dealer and account data is in DA Platform. Conversations, tickets and follow-ups are in HubSpot. Calls are in Dialpad. A one-way sync keeps HubSpot roughly current, but nobody gets one picture of a dealer, and every new idea means another HubSpot property or workflow.

DA-CRM puts that work next to the data it is about:

  • One record per dealership and group. It points at the real DA Platform account, so names, plans and trial status are never copied and never drift.
  • One timeline. Every call, email, chat, note, stage change, file, ticket and task, plus billing events from da-billing, lands in a single history per company and per contact.
  • Built around how we work. We have no sales team, no contracts and no outbound prospecting: all business is inbound and Customer Support turns trials into paying dealers. The product says Trial → Account setup & training → Paying, never "deal", "lead" or "prospect".
  • Staff only. It lives at /crm behind a DA-CRM permission. Dealers never see it.

HubSpot keeps its marketing role for now. DA-CRM takes over CRM, ticketing and workflows. Retiring HubSpot completely is a decision for after Phase 7.

How it is organised

Companies

There are four kinds: dealers, groups, vendors (paper and label suppliers, software vendors, associations) and feed suppliers. Only dealers have a pipeline stage, a trial clock and brands such as Chrysler, Jeep and Ford. Group pages list their member dealers. Any company can hold its own emails (for example support@), files such as feed-request PDFs, an owner and notes.

Team

There are two teams, Customer Support and Management. Tier 1, Tier 2, Feed support and AR/AP are capability tags on a person, not separate teams. Each member has working hours, a time zone, a photo, an email signature and their Dialpad line. DA-CRM admins manage the team and assign company owners.

Trial→Account setup & training→PayingCancelledLost

Every morning at 6:00 Pacific, the trial day is recalculated for each dealer in Trial. The timeline is noted at day 7, day 14, day 21 and expiry, and the dashboard lists the trials that end this week.

Tickets vs. tasks. A ticket is something that needs fixing. A task is something to do. Either one can be opened from a call, email, chat or company/contact record.

The Phase 1 database already includes the ticket and task tables, so those phases add screens rather than rework. Ticket and task counts already appear on every company and on the dashboard.

Phase 1 screens

  • Dashboard: calls today, trials expiring this week, unmatched calls, plus placeholders for tickets and tasks.
  • Dealers: tabs for Dealers, Groups and Partners. Each company page has a header (kind, brands, stage, owner, trial day, open tickets and tasks) and tabs for Timeline, Contacts, Calls, Files and Notes.
  • Contacts: searchable list, and a detail page with its timeline and click-to-call.
  • Calls: every Dialpad call, filterable by person, direction, outcome and date. Each call shows its recording, transcript and AI summary. Unmatched calls are assigned from here.
  • Trials: dealers in Trial and in Setup & training, with trial day, owner, last activity and days since last contact.
  • Team (admins): member cards and editing, the DA-CRM name and logo, and webhook subscribers.

Action items for Allan

In the steps below, "CC" means Claude Code: you hand that step to a Claude Code session, which does the server and database work. Steps 1, 3 and 4 can go to CC once you approve them. Steps 2, 5 and 6 need you.

1Approve, then apply the database migration

Review branch crm-phase-1 in da-platform. Paste supabase/migrations/179_crm_foundation.sql into the Supabase SQL editor and run it. It only adds new tables and creates a DA-CRM record for every existing dealer and group. CC then deploys the code and runs the back-fill (npm run crm:seed -- --all --apply), which sets up brands, team profiles, a few test contacts and the clearly labelled test partners.

2Add two new secrets

Add CRM_WEBHOOK_SECRET and CRM_EVENTS_TOKEN (two long random values) to /var/www/da-platform/.env.local, next to the Dialpad and Microsoft keys that are already there. Share both with da-billing.

3Register the Dialpad webhook

The URL is https://app.dealeraddendums.com/api/crm/dialpad/webhook, signed with DIALPAD_WEBHOOK_SECRET. CC can do it in one step: npm run crm:dialpad-setup -- --check --register --apply creates the webhook and a company-wide call-event subscription. Then make one test call and confirm it appears under Calls.

4Map each team member to their Dialpad user

Each team member needs their Dialpad user linked for click-to-call and call attribution. npm run crm:dialpad-setup -- --map --apply matches people by email. Anyone it can't match is set on the Team page (Dialpad user id + number). Until someone is mapped, their phone buttons open the phone app instead.

5Schedule three jobs in EasyCron

All three send the X-Cron-Secret header as today:

  • Trial day: /api/cron/crm-trial-day at 06:00 America/Los_Angeles
  • Dialpad catch-up: /api/cron/crm-dialpad-reconcile nightly
  • Webhook retries: /api/cron/crm-webhook-retry every 5 minutes

6Microsoft 365 app registration (for Phase 2, Inbox)

The app's ID, secret and tenant are already in .env.local. Before Phase 2, confirm in Entra ID that the app has Microsoft Graph application permissions for mail (read, send, and mark read/move). Have it limited to the support mailbox only (an Exchange application access policy), and note when the client secret expires.
