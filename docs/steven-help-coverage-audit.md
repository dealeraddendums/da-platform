## Retrieval findings (Part B)

**Method (as of 3a1112e, `lib/help-context.ts` `getRelevantArticlesScored`):** every request loads ALL published
articles with audience `dealer`/`all` (up to 500 — there are 8), scores each in memory (question word in title = 3,
in body = 1, stopwords removed, crude plural stem), and hands the top 4 to the model; if nothing scores, it falls
back to the first 4 by sort order (and the answer is effectively ungrounded).

**Full-set coverage: YES** for published dealer/all articles — no category filter, no cap below the set size.

**Unreachable by design / by setup:**
- `internal` articles (1: "Getting Dealers Into Platform 5.0 — Invites & Logins") — never retrieved, even when a
  super_admin chats with Steven. Fine for dealers; a gap if staff want Steven as an internal tool.
- `group` audience — Steven never retrieves these, even for group admins (the Help Center browse page DOES show
  them to group roles). No group articles exist yet, so nothing is lost today; it matters once group articles are written.
- Unpublished drafts (23) — invisible until published.
- Video-only articles (4) — reachable but contribute no text; Steven can only say the article exists.

"addendum"). Worth revisiting once there are 30+ articles.
